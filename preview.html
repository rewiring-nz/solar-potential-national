Disclaimer: This is a rough estimate, can make mistakes, and has clear limitations. Read more
  • The 3D data used to map rooftops comes from a different year (sometimes many years apart) from the imagery. Therefore sometimes buildings are missing, or buildings show where they have since been knocked down.
  • The solar irradiance data is based on satellite estimates that may not be perfectly accurate for an individual building’s sunlight conditions.
  • The panel placement, and detection of roof obstructions, requires reconciling 3D data, 2D data, and building outline records, which do not always match, so there are plenty of examples where panels can be placed incorrectly.
  • Solar economics change based on individual building consumption patterns, electricity contracts and more. These are only rough estimates, with customisable assumptions to show you how the estimate is made.

Self-consumption
Set by the site's daytime load, not by a share of output: a bigger array does not make the building use more. Whatever the load cannot absorb is exported, so the self-consumed share falls as systems grow.
